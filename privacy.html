CalFox Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Applies to the CalFox iOS app and its widgets

CalFox plans your day around your calendar, your sleep and how you feel. It runs on your iPhone. There is no CalFox account, no server of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your data, so we never sell or share it. If you connect with other CalFox users and invite them to events, the details you choose go to them through Apple's iCloud, never through us.

Data that stays on your device

Calendars. With your permission, CalFox reads the events in Apple Calendar, including Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars added there, so it can plan around what is already booked. It never adds, changes or deletes a calendar event by itself. When you tap Open in Calendar or Invite in Calendar on one of your calendar events, Apple's own event editor opens, and only what you change there is saved, to that event's calendar. You choose which calendars it reads.

Apple Health. With your permission, CalFox reads sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing, activity, workouts, mindful minutes and similar readings from Apple Health to plan a day you can do. It never writes to Health. Health readings are used on your device and are not stored in iCloud. If you turn on iCloud sync, your score history can say that a score moved because of your sleep or another Health reading, but not which reading it was.

What you enter. Your habits, goals, check-in answers, completed activities, scores and facts (the notes and limits on More → Privacy → Facts) live in the app's private storage on your device and in your device backups.

Your voice. When you tap the voice button to add something, your iPhone turns what you say into text on the device, with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is never sent anywhere and is not kept. If your iPhone cannot recognize speech on the device, CalFox does not offer voice and you type instead.

The widgets. The Up Next widget reads today's plan and today's all-day calendar events from the app's storage on your device. The fox widget reads today's plan, the kind of each item on it, each habit session's chance, today's Break windows with each Break's name and chance, your wake time and bedtime, tomorrow's first item, and your fox's name and how grown it is, from the same storage. The widgets send nothing anywhere.

iCloud sync (optional, off until you turn it on)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud in More, CalFox keeps a copy of what you enter, your scores and your facts (the notes and limits) in the private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple account, so your other devices show the same plan. Apple stores it inside your account; we cannot read it. Health readings and your Gemini key are not synced.

Invites (optional)

You can connect with another CalFox user by sending them a link from More → People. The link shows your first name and a picture of both foxes, which shows how grown your fox is, to anyone who has it, in Messages and when it is opened. Nothing else is shared until they tap Connect, and each link connects one person. On iOS 17 the link goes through Messages to the people you send it to: if you send it to a group, the first person to connect is the one connected and CalFox takes the others off, but until it does, what is in the shared space can reach their iPhones, though CalFox does not show it to them. So send it to one person. The two of you then share a private space in iCloud, kept in the iCloud account of whoever sent the link and counted toward that account's storage. CalFox writes into it your first name, your fox's name and how grown your fox is (it grows as you level up; your level itself is not shared), the events you invite each other to (title, colour, note, day, time, length, time zone and where) and your answers (Going, Maybe or No). On an event with several guests, each guest also sees the other guests' first names, fox names, how grown their foxes are, and answers. These details are encrypted on your iPhone before they go to iCloud, so only the two of you can read them; we cannot, and there is no server of ours. The link's first name and picture are not encrypted, since the link shows them before anyone connects. Nothing else is shared: not your Health readings, how you feel, your scores, levels or odds, your other events, habits or facts, or where you are. If an event you go to has the same name as one of your Builds, CalFox counts it for that Build on your iPhone; nothing about that is shared. Invites use iCloud even when Sync with iCloud is off. The titles, notes and places of events someone else made are never sent to Gemini. Remove in More → People ends a connection: the events you share leave both your days and the shared space is deleted. Thirty days after an event ends, each of you deletes from the shared space what you wrote for it, and a link nobody opened is deleted after 30 days.

Data that leaves your device: Gemini (optional)

CalFox can use Google's Gemini to word suggestions, read patterns, find habits you could do together, learn how long your activities take, tell what kind of thing a calendar event is, suggest what leads to a Break, check the times in a day it has planned and the days it picked for your habits in the coming week, and read what you add by typing or talking. This only happens if you paste your own Gemini API key into More. The key is kept in your device's Keychain, only on this device. With a key saved, CalFox sends short text straight from your iPhone to Google:

  • the names of your activities, the titles of calendar events (only those the phone cannot sort by itself, to tell what kind of thing each is, and never one on your Not asked about list), and how you feel as two words from a fixed list;
  • when a habit is ready to step up, its name, its length, how often you do it and the goal it builds to;
  • when CalFox checks a pattern in your habits, the habit's name and how often you did it, with and without something like a meeting the hour before (never a pattern found in your sleep);
  • when CalFox looks for two habits you could do at the same time, the names of your habits;
  • when your fox words an experiment, the habit's name and what the experiment tests, like a time of day;
  • when CalFox learns how long your activities and calendar events take and which days they fit, their names;
  • when you pick what leads to a Break, the Break's name, to suggest the states most likely to lead to a slip;
  • when you add something by typing or talking, the words you typed or said (as text, never audio), today's date and time zone, and the names of your habits, so CalFox can tell whether it is something you did, an event, a to-do or a habit and fill in its day and time. You see and can change everything it read before anything is added;
  • when CalFox checks the days and times it picked for your habits in the coming week, at most once a day: each habit's name and length, the day and time picked and the other days and times it could use, the busy times of each day without their titles, and the facts described in the next item. Gemini can only keep a pick or choose one of those other times; your iPhone checks every rule again before anything moves;
  • when CalFox checks a day it has planned, the names and times of what it planned for you (habits, meals, breaks and suggestions), the kinds and times of your calendar events, never their titles (for an event on your Not asked about list, only its time), and the facts marked with a dot on More → Privacy → Facts: your wake and wind-down times and when your weekends start, your work or school hours and whether you can step away, the day and time of a weekly calendar event you told your fox to plan around (never its title), your meal times, your caffeine and exercise limits, the days you do a habit on, and the likes, dislikes and things to avoid you typed in or said yes to when your fox asked. Never your goals, your conditions or anything from Health. This catches times that don't make sense, such as lunch late in the evening.

Nothing else is sent: no Health numbers, no audio, no calendar notes, attendees or locations, no name, no email, nor the title, note or place of an event someone invited you to. Google handles this text under Google's terms for your key. On Google's free tier, Google may use it to improve its products and people at Google may read it; on a paid plan it does not. Google requires Gemini API users to be 18 or older. More → Privacy lists what was sent lately, and the text of each ask. Everything in CalFox works without a key.

Notifications

Reminders for your Runup, your Rundown and your habits are scheduled on your device. They are optional and can be turned off in More or in iOS Settings. With iCloud sync on, Apple may send silent notifications to tell the app that new data is waiting in your iCloud.

When someone invites you, moves or cancels an event, CalFox shows a notification with the title, day and time, never the place or the note, and you can answer Accept, Maybe or Decline from it. Your answer goes to them through iCloud, as it does from the app. iCloud may send silent notifications to tell CalFox an invite changed. CalFox writes the notification itself on your iPhone, so the time is in your time zone. These silent notifications can come late, and they do not reach CalFox after you close it from the app switcher; the invite then shows the next time you open CalFox. Notifications for invites are asked for once, the first time you use invites, and can be turned off in iOS Settings; your fox still brings invites in the app.

TestFlight

If you test CalFox through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Deleting your data

In the app: More → Erase all my data deletes your habits, goals, scores, history and facts. With iCloud sync on, it deletes them from your other devices and your iCloud too. Erase all my data also removes you from the people you connected with and deletes the shared spaces kept in your iCloud. Remove your Gemini key in More, and turn off calendar and Health access in iOS Settings.

On your iPhone: deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the device. To delete an iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → CalFox. If you delete the app first, a shared space stays in iCloud until you remove it there.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

CalFox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The optional Gemini feature is for people 18 or older, under Google's terms.

Permissions the app asks for

Calendars (full access): plan around your events; an event changes only when you edit it yourself in Apple's event editor. Apple Health (read only): plan around your sleep and how you feel. Microphone and Speech Recognition: only while you tap the voice button to add something; the words are worked out on your iPhone and the audio is never sent. Notifications: optional reminders and invites. Each can be declined or turned off in iOS Settings. CalFox still works without them, with less to plan from.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
