CalFox Support

Help for the CalFox iOS app

Questions, bugs or ideas: email neal.falletta.cowden@gmail.com. If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot in CalFox and share it with Share Beta Feedback.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, tap Continue with email, then Sign in. Type your email and tap Forgot password?. We email you a link; if you don't see it, check your spam folder. Open the link, choose a new password, then sign in with it in CalFox.

I don't remember how I signed in

When you're signed out, CalFox shows only the way your account was made: Apple, Google or email. Sign in that way and everything is where you left it.

I can't get into my Apple or Google account

CalFox never sees your Apple or Google password. To get back into those accounts, use iforgot.apple.com for Apple or Google's account recovery, then sign in to CalFox again.

Signing in and the AI features

Signing in is optional. Everything works without an account except the three AI features, which you can see on your fox's page. Sign in with Apple, Google or an email and password to unlock them. An email account needs its address verified first: open the link we email you.

A new phone

Turn on Sync with iCloud in More, and sign in to the same Apple ID on your new phone. Open CalFox and sign in the way you did before, and your habits, to-dos and history come back.

My Google or Outlook calendar is missing

CalFox reads the calendars in Apple Calendar. Add the account in iOS Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account (on iOS 17: Settings → Calendar → Accounts). Then turn it on in CalFox under More → Calendars.

Reminders don't arrive

Check More → Daily reminders, then iOS Settings → Notifications → CalFox.

Adding the widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget (on iOS 17, tap + in the top corner). Search for CalFox and add Up Next or Fox Status.

Starting over

More → Run setup again walks through setup without deleting anything. More → Erase all my data deletes your habits, goals, scores and history.

Deleting your account

Signed in, tap Delete account at the top of More. It deletes your account, your habits, to-dos and history on your phone and in iCloud, and your connections with other people. Setup starts over.
