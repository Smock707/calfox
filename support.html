Help for the CalFox iOS app

Questions, bugs or ideas: email neal.falletta.cowden@gmail.com. If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in CalFox and share it with Share Beta Feedback.

My Google or Outlook calendar is missing

CalFox reads the calendars in Apple Calendar. Add the account in iOS Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account (on iOS 17: Settings → Calendar → Accounts). Then turn it on in CalFox under More → Calendars.

Reminders don't arrive

Check More → Daily reminders, then iOS Settings → Notifications → CalFox. You can change your Runup time in More.

Adding the Up Next widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget (on iOS 17, tap + in the top corner). Search for CalFox and add Up Next.

Using Gemini

Gemini is optional. Create an API key at Google AI Studio and paste it in More → Gemini. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent.

Starting over

More → Run setup again walks through setup without deleting anything. More → Erase all my data deletes your habits, goals, scores and history.
